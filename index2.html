E85 Score

E85 wins big on emissions (lowest CO₂/km of all fuels) and matches petrol's fast refuel time, but its higher running cost and lower mileage keep it from being an outright cost winner — a genuine eco-vs-economy paradox.

Fuel Type Comparison

CNG

  • ✅ Low cost/km (₹3.34)
  • ✅ Decent CO₂/km
  • ❌ 8-min refuel, limited stations
  • ❌ Boot space trade-off
🚗 Best for: high-mileage budget commuters

Diesel

  • ✅ Best highway mileage
  • ✅ Fast 5-min refuel
  • ❌ Highest maintenance/km
  • ❌ CO₂/km rises steeply with age
🚗 Best for: long-distance/highway driving

Electric (EV)

  • ✅ Lowest cost/km (₹1.75)
  • ✅ Lowest maintenance/km
  • ❌ 45-min charge time
  • ❌ Lowest per-charge range
🚗 Best for: urban commutes with home charging

Petrol (E20)

  • ✅ Widely available, fast refuel
  • ✅ No engine modification needed
  • ❌ Highest cost/km (₹6.21)
  • ❌ Cost/km climbs after 5-6 yrs
🚗 Best for: mixed daily use, easy fueling access

E85 (Flex-Fuel)

  • ✅ Lowest CO₂/km of all fuels
  • ✅ Fast 5-min refuel
  • ❌ Highest cost/km (₹6.37)
  • ❌ Lower mileage per litre
🚗 Best for: eco-conscious flex-fuel owners
