# Day 14 - Job Red Flag Detector

## Job Role
**Junior Full Stack Developer**

## Overall Risk Score
**89/100 🔴**

## Top Red Flags

1. "Junior" role requiring 5+ years experience — Severity 10
   - Contradictory role title, may indicate lower pay for senior-level work.

2. 12 unrelated tech stacks required — Severity 9
   - Unrealistic expectations for one developer.

3. "24/7 support when needed" — Severity 9
   - High burnout risk due to unpaid on-call work.

4. Fake remote job with hidden relocation clause — Severity 9
   - Remote role but relocation to Bengaluru required after probation.

5. No salary disclosed — Severity 8
   - Lack of transparency.

6. "Work hard, play hard", "Family culture", "Thrive under pressure" — Severity 8
   - Possible hustle culture and poor work-life balance.

7. Junior expected to lead clients, design architecture, and mentor others — Severity 8
   - Responsibilities don't match the role title.

8. Early-stage startup with 12 employees and long working hours — Severity 7
   - High business and job stability risk.

---

## Positive Signals

- AI/ML exposure may improve technical skills.
- Small team can provide faster learning opportunities.
- Early-stage startup may offer equity or career growth.

## Final Verdict

**❌ Avoid**

Proceed only if you urgently need experience and ensure all employment terms are documented in writing.

---

## Interview Questions

1. Why is this a Junior role requiring 5+ years of experience?
2. Is relocation to Bengaluru mandatory after probation?
3. How does the 24/7 support policy work? Is on-call compensated?
4. Which technologies will I actually use during the first 3 months?
5. What is the company's funding status and employee retention rate?
